# Stakeholders

## Overview

The insights generated from this project are intended to support multiple teams responsible for managing marketplace performance, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

Each stakeholder group has different business objectives and therefore requires different analytical insights to support data-driven decision making.


## Executive Team

### Responsibilities

The Executive Team is responsible for defining the overall business strategy, monitoring marketplace performance, and identifying opportunities for sustainable growth.

### Business Questions

- Is the marketplace growing sustainably?
- Which business areas contribute most to revenue?
- What are the biggest opportunities for improving customer experience?
- Which operational issues have the greatest business impact?
- Where should future investments be prioritised?

### How This Project Helps

This project provides a high-level view of marketplace performance by combining financial, operational, and customer-related metrics to support strategic decision making.


## Growth Team

### Responsibilities

The Growth Team focuses on increasing marketplace revenue by improving customer acquisition, customer retention, and purchasing behaviour.

### Business Questions

- Which customers generate the highest value?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which purchasing patterns create growth opportunities?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- Where can revenue growth be improved?

### How This Project Helps

The analysis identifies revenue drivers, customer purchasing behaviour, and product performance, enabling the Growth Team to make data-driven decisions that support marketplace expansion.

## Operations Team

### Responsibilities

The Operations Team is responsible for ensuring efficient order processing, delivery performance, and overall marketplace operations.

### Business Questions

- Which orders experience delivery delays?
- Which regions experience operational inefficiencies?
- How does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction?
- Which operational bottlenecks should be addressed first?

### How This Project Helps

The project analyses order fulfilment, delivery timelines, freight costs, and operational performance to identify opportunities for improving efficiency and customer experience.

## Seller Success Team

### Responsibilities

The Seller Success Team works with marketplace sellers to improve product quality, operational performance, and customer satisfaction.

### Business Questions

- Which sellers consistently perform well?
- Which sellers generate poor customer experiences?
- Which sellers contribute the most revenue?
- Which sellers require operational support?

### How This Project Helps

The analysis evaluates seller performance using revenue, customer reviews, delivery performance, and sales activity to support evidence-based seller management.

## Summary

Although each stakeholder group has different priorities, they all rely on the same underlying objective:

> **Identify high-impact opportunities to improve marketplace revenue and customer experience through data-driven decision making.**

This project is designed to provide analytical insights that support both strategic planning and day-to-day business operations across the marketplace.
