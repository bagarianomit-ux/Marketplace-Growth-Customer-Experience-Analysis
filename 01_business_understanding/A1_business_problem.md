# Business Problem

## Background

Olist is one of Brazil's largest e-commerce marketplaces, connecting thousands of independent sellers with customers across a wide range of product categories. The platform manages the complete marketplace lifecycle, including customer orders, seller operations, payments, product catalogues, logistics, and post-purchase reviews.

As the marketplace continues to grow, maintaining a balance between revenue growth and customer satisfaction becomes increasingly challenging. Decisions related to seller performance, product assortment, order fulfilment, pricing, and delivery operations directly influence both business performance and customer experience.

To support these decisions, marketplace leaders require reliable, data-driven insights rather than relying on assumptions.


## Business Problem

Although the marketplace captures extensive operational and transactional data, it does not explain why business performance changes or where the greatest improvement opportunities exist.

Key business questions include:

- Which products and categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which sellers consistently deliver high-quality customer experiences?
- What operational factors contribute to delivery delays?
- Which customer segments generate the highest long-term value?
- How do payment methods influence purchasing behaviour?
- Where can operational improvements increase customer satisfaction while supporting revenue growth?

Answering these questions requires integrating data from multiple operational systems and transforming it into meaningful business insights.


## Project Goal

The primary goal of this project is to identify high-impact opportunities to improve marketplace revenue and customer experience through comprehensive analysis of customer behaviour, seller performance, product trends, operational efficiency, and marketplace transactions.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow, beginning with business understanding and progressing through data understanding, data validation, database design, SQL analysis, dashboard development, and business recommendations.

## Project Scope

This analysis focuses on the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset, which captures marketplace activity between 2016 and 2018.

The project includes analysis across multiple business areas, including:

- Customers
- Orders
- Products
- Sellers
- Payments
- Reviews
- Product Categories
- Delivery Performance

The objective is not only to describe historical performance but also to generate actionable business recommendations that support marketplace growth and improve the overall customer experience.


## Business Impact

The insights generated throughout this project are intended to support data-driven decision making by helping stakeholders:

- Improve marketplace revenue performance.
- Enhance customer satisfaction.
- Optimise seller performance.
- Identify operational bottlenecks.
- Improve delivery efficiency.
- Support strategic business planning through evidence-based recommendations.
