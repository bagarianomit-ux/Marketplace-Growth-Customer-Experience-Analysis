# Business Objectives

## Overview

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw marketplace data into actionable business insights that support informed decision making across the Olist marketplace.

The analysis focuses on identifying opportunities to improve marketplace revenue, enhance customer experience, and optimise operational performance through evidence-based recommendations.


## Primary Business Objective

Identify high-impact opportunities to improve marketplace revenue and customer experience by analysing customer behaviour, seller performance, product performance, payment behaviour, and order fulfilment processes.


## Supporting Business Objectives

### 1. Understand Customer Behaviour

Analyse purchasing patterns, repeat purchase behaviour, customer distribution, and review activity to better understand how customers interact with the marketplace.


### 2. Evaluate Seller Performance

Measure seller performance using sales volume, revenue contribution, delivery performance, and customer review scores to identify high-performing sellers and areas requiring improvement.


### 3. Analyse Product Performance

Identify the products and product categories that contribute most to marketplace revenue and sales performance.

### 4. Assess Operational Efficiency

Evaluate order processing and delivery performance to identify operational bottlenecks that may affect customer satisfaction.


### 5. Understand Payment Behaviour

Analyse payment methods, payment instalments, and transaction values to understand purchasing behaviour and payment preferences.


### 6. Evaluate Customer Experience

Investigate the relationship between delivery performance, order fulfilment, and customer reviews to identify factors associated with post-purchase customer satisfaction.


## Expected Outcomes

By achieving these objectives, the project aims to:

- Identify key drivers of marketplace revenue.
- Highlight opportunities to improve customer satisfaction.
- Detect operational inefficiencies affecting order fulfilment.
- Support data-driven seller performance management.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve marketplace performance.
- Deliver interactive dashboards that support ongoing business monitoring and decision making.

## Alignment with Project Deliverables

These business objectives guide every stage of the analytics workflow, including:

- Business Understanding
- Data Understanding
- Data Audit & Profiling
- Data Quality Assessment
- Database Design
- Data Loading & Validation
- SQL Business Analysis
- Advanced Business Analysis
- Dashboard Development (Power BI)

Each stage of the workflow builds upon the previous one, ensuring that every project deliverable contributes directly to achieving the business objectives defined above.
