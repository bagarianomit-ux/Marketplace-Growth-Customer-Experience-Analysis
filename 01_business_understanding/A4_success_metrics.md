# Success Metrics

## Overview

The success of this project is measured by its ability to generate meaningful insights that support data-driven decision making across the Olist marketplace.

The following business metrics represent the key performance indicators (KPIs) used to evaluate marketplace performance, customer experience, and operational efficiency.



## Revenue & Sales Performance

These metrics evaluate the marketplace's financial performance and overall sales activity.

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Seller
- Revenue Trends Over Time


## Customer Metrics

These metrics measure customer behaviour and engagement across the marketplace.

- Total Customers
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Average Orders per Customer
- Customer Geographic Distribution
- Customer Review Score Distribution


## Seller Performance Metrics

These metrics evaluate seller contribution and operational performance.

- Total Active Sellers
- Sales per Seller
- Revenue per Seller
- Average Review Score by Seller
- Seller Delivery Performance


## Product Performance Metrics

These metrics identify the products and categories that drive marketplace performance.

- Best-Selling Products
- Best-Selling Categories
- Product Sales Distribution
- Product Revenue Contribution


## Operational Metrics

These metrics measure fulfilment efficiency and delivery performance.

- Average Delivery Time
- Orders Delivered On Time
- Orders Delivered Late
- Average Freight Cost
- Order Status Distribution


## Payment Metrics

These metrics provide insights into customer payment behaviour.

- Payment Method Distribution
- Average Payment Value
- Average Number of Installments
- Payment Value Distribution


## Customer Experience Metrics

These metrics evaluate post-purchase customer satisfaction.

- Average Review Score
- Review Score Distribution
- Delivery Performance vs Review Score
- Delivery Delays vs Customer Satisfaction


## Project Success Criteria

This project will be considered successful if it:

- Identifies actionable opportunities to improve marketplace revenue.
- Identifies factors influencing customer satisfaction.
- Highlights operational inefficiencies affecting marketplace performance.
- Provides evidence-based recommendations supported by data.
- Delivers interactive dashboards that communicate insights clearly.
- Demonstrates a complete end-to-end analytics workflow from business understanding to business recommendations.


## Alignment with Stakeholders

These success metrics enable different stakeholder groups to monitor the areas most relevant to their responsibilities:

| Stakeholder | Primary Success Metrics |
|------------|-------------------------|
| Executive Team | Revenue Growth, Customer Satisfaction, Operational Performance |
| Growth Team | Revenue, Repeat Purchase Rate, Product Performance |
| Operations Team | Delivery Performance, Freight Cost, Order Status |
| Seller Success Team | Seller Performance, Customer Reviews, Sales Activity |

Together, these metrics provide a balanced framework for evaluating marketplace performance and measuring progress toward the project's business objectives.
